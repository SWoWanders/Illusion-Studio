Shape Weave

Turn a portrait into a tiled pattern of shapes — legible as pure geometry up close, and as a face from across the room. Each cell keeps a fixed color pairing; only the size of its inner shape shifts to reveal more or less of the paired color, so the decorative rhythm never breaks.
